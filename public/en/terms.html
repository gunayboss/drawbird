1. The service

2. Age

If you are under 13, use DrawBird with a parent or teacher. Do not put your real name, school or contact details in your artist name.

3. Drawing rules

  • Draw birds. It doesn’t have to look perfect, but the sky is for birds.

  • Do not post someone else’s copyrighted character or logo as your own work.

A new artist’s first two birds become public after a moderator approves them; after that their birds fly straight away. Inappropriate words in bird, artist and flock names are blocked automatically. Birds that break the rules may be removed without notice; devices that keep breaking them may be blocked from posting.
