Frequently asked questions

Does DrawBird cost money?

No, it's completely free. No payments, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can draw the moment you open the page. Your browser makes a random code for you. We don't ask for an email or a password.

Why isn't my bird showing in the sky?

Only up to 100 birds fly in the sky at once, so in a crowd yours may have to wait in line. Pick “Newest” from the Show menu and your bird comes to the front. If your bird got a few reports, it comes down from the sky until an admin looks at it.

Why isn't my bird flapping?

The part above the dashed line on the canvas flaps like a wing, and the part below stays as the body. Try drawing the wings above the line. The drawing lessons show this step by step.

How do I color the inside of a shape?

Choose the bucket from the tools, pick a color and tap inside the shape. A closed shape fills in one go. If there's a gap in the edge, the paint spills out, so close the shape.

How many birds can I draw?

You can have at most 8 birds at the same time. For a new bird, you can delete an old one from Profile.

Can I see my birds on my phone too?

Yes. Copy the recovery code in Profile, open DrawBird on your phone and paste it into Profile → Sign in with code. Don't share the code with anyone.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your device code is deleted and you can't reach your birds from that device. If you saved the recovery code beforehand, you can enter it again.

I saw an inappropriate drawing, what do I do?

Tap the bird and press "Report". A bird that gets three reports (two on its first day) comes down from the sky right away and goes to the admin.

How do I delete my data?

The "Delete all my data" button in Profile permanently deletes your birds, votes, reports, flocks and artist name.

Who owns the rights to my drawings?

You do. DrawBird only gets permission to show them on the site and as a preview in share links. We don't sell your drawing or print it on products. Details in the terms of use.

Which languages does it support?

The game comes in 11 languages: Turkish, English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Indonesian. The language is picked based on your browser, and you can change it from the menu.

Can I add it to my phone like an app?

Yes. Choose "Add to Home Screen" from your browser menu. DrawBird opens like an app.

Is the source code open?

Yes. It is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 license.
