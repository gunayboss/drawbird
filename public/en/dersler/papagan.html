How to draw a parrot

Easy

The most colorful birds in the sky are parrots. In this lesson we will draw a red, yellow and blue macaw. Be bold with your colors.

The dashed line is the wing line in the DrawBird canvas: everything above it flaps, everything below it stays as the body.

Step by step

1. Body

Under the dashed line, draw a long egg, slightly tilted. A parrot's body gets thinner toward the tail.

2. Head

Add a round head at the right end of the body. A macaw has a bare white patch on its face, so leave the area around the eye white.

3. Beak

The beak is what makes a parrot a parrot. Draw a big, hooked beak curving downward. The upper beak closes over the lower one.

4. Wing

ABOVE the dashed line, draw a wide wing. Make the shoulder yellow and the tip blue. Without those color bands, it's not a parrot.

5. Long tail

On the left of the body, draw a long, thin, pointed tail as long as the body and paint the tip blue.

Done!

Parrot is ready. Now draw it in DrawBird, give it a name and let it loose in the sky.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the beak small and straight. A parrot's beak is big and hooked.
  • Using one color. The color bands on the wing are what identify a parrot.
  • Drawing the tail short. A macaw's tail is as long as its body.

What you should know about Parrot

  • There are around 400 species of parrots in the world. Most live in tropical forests.
  • On their feet, two toes point forward and two point back. They can hold food like a hand.
  • Big parrots can live more than 50 years.
  • Some can imitate hundreds of words and sounds.
