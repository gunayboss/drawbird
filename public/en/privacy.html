Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Translation of the Turkish original. Turkish data protection law (KVKK) applies; see the KVKK notice (Turkish).

What we store

  • Your drawings and bird names. Every bird you post is visible in the public sky and ranking.
  • Your artist name, if you set one on your Profile. You can leave it empty.
  • Your votes, reports and flocks, to calculate scores and lists.
  • A device code. Your browser creates a random code and keeps it. The server only stores a one-way hash of it. We never ask for an email, password or real name.

Abuse protection

To stop spam and fake votes, your IP address is never stored as is. Only a keyed, irreversible hash is kept: up to two days for counting new accounts and up to one hour for posting limits. The hash of the network you post from is kept with your account and is used only to stop new accounts from that network for 24 hours if your account is banned for breaking the rules.

Review

A new artist’s first two birds are seen by a moderator (image and names) before they become public.

Visit counting

A daily counter goes up by one per visit. It uses no cookies and records nothing about who you are; it only keeps “visits today”.

What we don’t do

We currently use no ads, ad cookies, location, contact lists or third-party analytics. Fonts are served from our own server. If ads are added, this page will be updated and your consent will be asked first where required.

Who processes the data

The site and database run on Cloudflare. If a bird needs review, its image and names are sent to the moderator’s Telegram account. We do not sell or hand over your data to anyone else.

Deleting

Delete your birds one by one on your Profile, or press Delete all my data there to permanently remove your birds, votes, reports, flocks and artist name.

Rights in your drawings

Your drawings are yours. Posting a bird gives DrawBird a free, non-exclusive permission to show it on the site (including pages with ads) and as a preview in share links. DrawBird does not sell your drawing, print it on products or use it elsewhere. The permission ends when you delete the bird.
