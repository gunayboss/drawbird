How to play

In DrawBird you draw a bird and it flaps its wings in one sky shared with everyone else’s birds. No sign-up: open the page and start drawing.

Draw your bird

  1. Make the beak face right. Birds fly both ways; a right-facing drawing is mirrored automatically when it turns. Drew it the wrong way? Press Flip.
  2. Put the wings above the dashed line. The dashed line across the canvas is the wing line. Everything above it opens and closes like wings; everything below stays still as the body.
  3. Use a thick pen. Birds get small in the sky. A size of 6 or more reads much better than thin lines.
  4. Add colour. Besides the nine colours there is a custom colour picker. White shines on the night sky.
  5. Name it and fly. Give your bird a name of up to 24 characters and press fly!.

Your first birds

To keep DrawBird friendly for all ages, a new artist’s first two birds are checked by a moderator before everyone can see them. Until then only you see them in the sky. After two approved birds, your next birds fly straight away.

In the sky

  • Choose newest, most loved or random birds, 1 to 100 birds at a time, and the speed.
  • Feed the birds: Shift + click or right-click on a computer; on a phone turn on Feed mode and tap. Nearby birds fly over and peck.
  • Tap a bird to see its name and artist, vote, or copy its link.

Votes and ranking

Give a bird ▲ or ▼; pressing again takes your vote back. The Ranking page sorts by Rising (new and loved), Score, Date or Random.

Flocks

Create a flock on your Profile, add birds you like from the ranking, then watch only that flock in the sky or share its link. Set it to “Only me” to keep it private.

Your account and other devices

DrawBird never asks for an email or password. Your birds are tied to this browser. To continue on another device, copy the recovery code from your Profile and paste it under Sign in with code there. Never share the code: whoever has it can delete your birds.

Limits

  • Up to 8 birds and 12 flocks per person.
  • A bird reported three times (twice on its first day) leaves the sky and goes to a moderator. See the terms.
